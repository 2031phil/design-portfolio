Background

During my time at montamo GmbH, besides the project management software and the field app, I was also working on the company website here and there and improving it. When the business model shifted towards franchising in early 2026, I was tasked with creating a new landing page to attract potential franchise owner leads.

I worked on this project together with one of the founders, Ole, who gave me an outline of the content he envisioned on this site. It was then my job to make this content look as appealing as possible to our target audience of young, ambitious master craftsmen. The challenge of this project was a time constraint of only two weeks to go from idea to deployment, since a craftsmanship exhibition was coming up and we wanted to launch it before then.

Competitive Analysis

smalt

Smalt’s website is highly effective because it focuses on clarity, outcomes, and trust. The homepage quickly communicates the value proposition, reinforces it with specific performance metrics and success stories, and guides visitors through a simple conversion-focused journey. The key takeaway from this example is to emphasize the business and lifestyle outcomes of ownership, support claims with measurable results, and clearly showcase the support system available to franchisees. Incorporating audience-specific messaging, strong social proof, and transparent information about investment and growth potential can help create a more compelling and conversion-driven experience.

handly

Handly’s website is particularly strong at positioning itself as a complete business-growth partner rather than just a software provider. The messaging consistently reinforces a simple promise: roofers should focus on roofing while Handly handles lead generation, administration, technology, recruiting, and operational support. The site uses strong proof points, partner testimonials, revenue-focused success stories, and clear before and after comparisons to demonstrate business impact. Emphasizing how the franchise removes common operational burdens, showcasing measurable success metrics, and presenting ownership as a partnership backed by proven systems creates a more compelling value proposition for prospective franchisees.

The Design Stage

Taking what I’d learned from studying our competitors, I created a structure for the page that focuses on communicating our value proposition and showcasing the advantages of working with us instead of alone.

The design itself is based on the updated brand guidelines (a project I’d worked on as well) and on the minimalistic and modern feel that our website already had.

I started the design in Figma by importing our design system and then building some bespoke components based on them. For text I used the standard montamo font “Geist” and our display font “PP Telegraf” to create a special visual focal point for headlines. All icons come from Shadcn.

Keeping the design simple for the most part helped me meet the tight deadline and focus more on the placement of content. It also made the process of making the layout responsive and mobile-friendly faster.

Working with GSAP

One of the things I learned from our competitors’ sites was the use of subtle scrolling and hover animations to engage the visitor and create a feeling of quality. Shortly before the end of the deadline I found myself with a bit more time left over which motivated me to try my hand at animation. This was only a “cherry on top” kinda thing and not a requirement from Ole, so I only considered it after I had finished everything else.

Since I was deploying the site via Webflow, it was easy to add scrolling and hovering animations through the built in GSAP extension. It took some playing around with different cues, values and easing curves until I got a result that I liked and that I thought would improve user experience. However, in then end I was really happy with the result and so were the other stakeholders.

Project Takeaways

Working with someone else’s content

On most of my projects so far I was responsible for the exact content and copy, but this project taught me to work with someone else’s text and ideas. This was challenging in some regards as it was not always possible to combine my design idea with certain content. However, through compromises and small adjustments this challenge was overcome at the end.

Working efficiently with Webflow

This project was the first where I had to use Webflow and it took me some time to figure out how it works and how to use it efficiently. Especially the way classes and responsive design are handled was a bit challenging for me at first.

The power of subtle animations

With tools like GSAP, adding high quality, satisfying animations to a website is really not that difficult anymore. It took me less than a day to apply it to this site, but it made it so much better already. However, one should keep in mind that animation will not magically save an otherwise poorly designed page and even on a well designed page, animations done wrong can decrease the user experience, especially for certain user groups with disabilities.
